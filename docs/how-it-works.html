Web3Tube · ranking and sources

How Web3Tube ranks crypto videos

Web3Tube ranks a video by how often credible people in builder and research communities have recommended it, across years and across communities. Views, likes on YouTube and follower counts are never used.

Build of 3 November 2026. 1,680 videos in the catalog; every mentioned video that still exists has been labelled.

The pipeline

Each step writes a file the next one reads, and each can be rerun without repeating finished work.

Crawl the communities

Public Discourse forums (newest posts first) and Hacker News via the Algolia API. Each post keeps its author, the author's forum trust level, date, likes and the thread it sits in.

make crawl · make social

Find the videos

Every YouTube link is normalised to its 11-character video id. The sentences around the link and any direct replies to the post are kept as the mention's context.

make extract

Fetch YouTube details

Title, channel, description, duration, captions flag, and whether the video is public and embeddable, from the YouTube Data API. Nothing is downloaded. Deleted and private videos drop out here.

make enrich

Label every mention with Claude

One Claude Haiku call per video sees its metadata and all its mentions. It labels each mention, quotes the evidence word for word, flags self-promotion, and judges the video's topics, technical depth, audience, shill risk and whether it is still current.

make classify

Score and filter

Six weighted signals, then anti-shilling penalties, then the inclusion rules below. The result is web/catalog.json.

make score

Serve a static site

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript reading the catalog. No login; preferences stay in the browser. Every video shows the posts that put it there.

make serve

Conference archives are used as seeds: 21,305 talks from Devcon / Ethereum Foundation, ETHGlobal and the Zero Knowledge podcast. A seed marks a video as a conference talk and adds a small bonus, but a seed alone never puts a video in the catalog.

How each mention is labelled

A link is not an endorsement. Most forum links are meeting recordings, announcements or passing references, so Claude reads the words around each link and picks one label. The number is how much that label counts toward a recommendation.

Recommendation

× 1.0

The author endorses it or relies on it as a trusted source: "great talk on X", "this explains it well".

Neutral reference

× 0.35

Cited without a judgement: call recordings, "here is the recording", reading lists.

Criticism

× 0

Says the video is wrong, misleading or outdated. Counts against it in discussion quality.

Promotion

× 0, penalised

Marketing, shilling, referral spam, or advertising one's own product or event.

Unrelated

× 0

The link is incidental, or the text says nothing about the video.

Separately, a mention is flagged as self-promotion when the author appears to be the speaker or uploader. Those count at a quarter of their weight.

How much one voice counts

Each person gets one vote per video, their strongest mention. The weight of that vote is:

vote = label weight × strength (0.3–1.0, from Claude)
     × author credibility
     × community weight
     × 0.25 if self-promotion

author credibility = 0.7 × forum trust level   (TL0 0.3 · TL1 0.5 · TL2 0.75 · TL3 0.9 · TL4 1.0; staff +0.1)
                   + 0.3 × how established     (log of the author's posts with videos, full at 50)

Votes are summed per community with diminishing returns, and no single community may supply more than 60% of a video's recommendation weight. Each author who promoted the video subtracts 0.5.

The six signals

Each signal is a number from 0 to 1. The score is their weighted sum. These weights are the starting proposal and have not been tuned against real outcomes yet.

Independent credible recommendations30%

The vote total above, saturating so that the first few strong voices matter most.

Technical depth25%

Claude's judgement of the video from its title, description and the discussion around it.

Discussion quality15%

Recommendations against criticism, each weighted by strength and author credibility. 0.5 means neutral.

Recommended across years15%

Distinct calendar years with an endorsing voice. Four or more years scores full marks.

Captions and still current10%

Half for having captions, half for Claude's "still current" judgement. Transcripts are not fetched, so this is a proxy.

Engagement on the recommending posts5%

Likes on the forum posts that recommend the video, log-scaled. YouTube views are never used.

Anti-shilling controls

  • One vote per author. Posting the same link ten times counts once.
  • Bursts collapse. Three or more low-trust accounts posting the same video within three days count as one.
  • Community cap. No single community supplies more than 60% of a video's recommendation weight.
  • Promotion subtracts. Each promoting author removes 0.5 of recommendation weight; self-promotion counts at 25%.
  • Sponsor language. Phrases like "use my code", "referral", "airdrop", "100x" or "price prediction" in the description cut the score by 20%.
  • Shill risk. The score is multiplied by (1 − 0.7 × Claude's shill-risk estimate).
  • No popularity inputs. YouTube views, subscriber counts and follower counts are never read.

What gets into the catalog

  • The video is still public and embeddable on YouTube.
  • Claude has labelled its mentions.
  • It is about crypto: its title, channel, description or Claude's summary names a crypto term as a whole word (about 100 terms, such as zk-SNARK, rollup, Zcash or proof of stake). General communities like Hacker News also link plenty of unrelated videos; this filter removed 6,607 of them.
  • At least one independent recommendation, or at least three independent neutral references.
  • A final score of at least 0.22.

Of 28,649 mentioned videos, 1,680 made the catalog. Most of the rest were left out for having too few independent voices (14,845), being off-topic (6,607) or no longer being on YouTube (4,848).

Separate shelves

A second, small Claude pass tags each catalog video's stance. Videos that argue against crypto or the industry (39 of them, such as James Mickens' "Blockchains Are a Bad Idea") go on a "Critiques & skeptics" shelf. Market and trading content (134 videos) goes on a labelled Markets shelf. Neither appears on the builder shelves, but both can still be found through search.

Known gaps, on hold until after testing

blocked

Reddit. Reddit returns HTTP 403 to anonymous requests. It needs a Reddit API app and OAuth to include.

unreachable

Aptos, Avalanche, Filecoin forums. Rate-limited or not answering. Worth retrying later.

partial

Zcash and Cardano forums. Stopped early for time. They have their newest posts; older years are missing.

skew

Cardano, Zcash and Hacker News supply most catalog entries. Cardano's weight is already lowered to 0.4. Ethereum research forums produce fewer but stronger entries. Weights per community should be reviewed against real use.
